How It Works

  1. Create a mapping with a unique masked ID and your target URL
  2. Share the generated masked URL (no DNS setup needed!)
  3. Anyone visiting your masked URL will see the target content
  4. Track clicks and manage mappings from this panel

Example:

{{ request.host_url }}m/mybrand → proxies to → https://myapp.vercel.app
